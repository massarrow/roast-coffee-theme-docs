# ROAST Coffee — Shopify Theme Documentation

**Version 1.0.0**

ROAST Coffee is a premium Shopify theme designed for specialty coffee brands, roasters, cafés, and coffee retailers.

The theme combines an editorial visual style with flexible Shopify Online Store 2.0 sections, product selling features, collection layouts, customer accounts, cart functionality, gift cards, search, blog content, and responsive mobile design.

---

## 1. Getting Started

ROAST Coffee is designed to work with Shopify's Online Store 2.0 theme editor.

After installing the theme:

1. Open **Online Store → Themes** in your Shopify admin.
2. Locate **ROAST Coffee**.
3. Select **Customize**.
4. Configure the global theme settings.
5. Configure your navigation menus.
6. Add or edit homepage sections.
7. Add your products and collections.
8. Review the storefront on desktop and mobile.

---

## 2. Installing the Theme

After purchasing or downloading ROAST Coffee:

1. Open your Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Online Store → Themes**.
3. Select **Add theme**.
4. Upload the ROAST Coffee theme ZIP file.
5. Wait for Shopify to finish uploading the theme.
6. Open **Customize** to configure the theme.

Always create a backup of your current theme before replacing an existing production theme.

## 30. Troubleshooting

### The logo does not appear

Check:

1. Theme editor → Branding.
2. Confirm a logo image has been uploaded.
3. Confirm the logo width is appropriate.
4. Save the theme.
5. Refresh the storefront.

### A menu link does not work

Check:

1. Shopify → Content → Menus.
2. Open the assigned menu.
3. Verify the destination.
4. Save the menu.
5. Test the link again.

### Products do not appear in a collection

Check:

1. Product status.
2. Collection conditions.
3. Product availability.
4. Sales channel availability.
5. Inventory settings where applicable.

### Product options do not appear correctly

Check the product's options and variants in Shopify Admin.

If swatches are being used, verify that the product/category information supports Shopify's swatch functionality.

### Newsletter signup does not work

Check:

1. The newsletter section is enabled.
2. The form is configured correctly.
3. The customer email is valid.
4. Shopify customer marketing settings are configured.

### Contact form does not appear

Verify that the correct Contact page/template is being used and that the contact section is enabled.
